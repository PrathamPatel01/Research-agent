## 📌 Summary
A Large Language Model (LLM) is a deep-learning algorithm that utilizes massive amounts of parameters and training data to understand and predict text, enabling it to process, generate, and interact with human-like text. LLMs have various applications across industries, including automated content creation and language translation. They are built on deep neural networks and can learn patterns, grammar, and context from text.

## 🔑 Key Developments
- LLMs use deep learning techniques and massive datasets to understand and generate human-like text.
- They can be used for automated content creation, answering questions, writing content, and translating languages.
- The process of training an LLM involves pretraining with a vast dataset of text from various sources.
